Why most teams haven't switched agents on

The agents are easy to buy. Trusting one in production is the hard part.

Trust

Vendors publish accuracy numbers, but almost none are independently checked. Few teams can tell whether the agent's answer was right.

Foundations

Without clean telemetry, current runbooks and clear service ownership, an agent is guessing.

Control

Who approves an agent's action? What data leaves your cloud? Who is accountable when it's wrong?

What we do

Start with an assessment, or bring us in for one piece. Every engagement is scoped to a fixed outcome.

Readiness Assessment

2 weeks

We review your telemetry, runbooks, on-call load and access model, then tell you where an agent will help first and what has to be fixed before it can.

Vendor-Neutral Agent Pilot

4 to 6 weeks

We pick two to four agents from the list below and replay them in read-only mode against 20 to 30 of your past incidents. You get a scorecard: how often each agent found the real cause, how fast, and what it would cost to run.

Guardrails & Governance

Least-privilege access, approval gates, dry-run modes, rate limits and audit trails. Then we red-team the agent itself: prompt injection through logs and tickets, over-broad permissions, data leaving your cloud.

Agentic DevOps Builds

Agents for Terraform plan review, CI failure triage, drift detection, Kubernetes troubleshooting and cloud cost.

Agent Ops retainer

Once agents are live, we keep them tuned: track their accuracy, update guardrails as your stack changes, and decide with you when each one earns more responsibility.

How we work: the trust ladder

Agents earn autonomy one step at a time.

Observe

Read-only. The agent investigates alongside your on-call engineers.

Recommend

It proposes a diagnosis and a fix. People decide.

Act with approval

It runs approved actions after a named person signs off.

Limited autonomy

It handles a defined set of safe actions on its own, with full audit.

A step unlocks only after the one before it has proven itself on measured results.

FAQ

Which tools do you work with?

Any of the agents listed above, plus whatever you're already trialling. We recommend what scored best on your incidents.

Yes. A lot of teams buy one and leave it in read-only mode. We measure it against your incidents, fix what it trips on, and move it up the ladder when the numbers support it.

Will an agent touch production?

Only with your approval, one step at a time. Pilots run read-only.

Does our data leave Canada?

That's your choice. Self-hosted and open-model options keep it in Canada.
